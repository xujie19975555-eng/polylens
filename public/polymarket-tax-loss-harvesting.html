In the US, realized capital losses on prediction-market ERC-1155 shares offset your winning Polymarket (and crypto/stock) capital gains dollar-for-dollar, plus up to $3,000 against ordinary income if net losses exceed gains.

2. Why Raw Polygon Wallet Syncs Drop $0 Losing Redemptions

Standard block-explorer importers miss Polymarket losses for two structural reasons:

  2. Proxy vs. EOA split: Polymarket executes fills through your proxy contract address while your funds bridge through your EOA wallet. Syncing only the EOA misses ERC-1155 inventory lots entirely.
