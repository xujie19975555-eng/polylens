1. FIFO Cost Basis & Acquisition Dates ("Various" for Multi-Lot Disposals)

Under First-In, First-Out (FIFO) accounting, every Polymarket BUY opens a USDC cost-basis lot stamped with its exact UTC acquisition timestamp for that specific market and outcome token.

  • Single-date match: If a SELL or REDEEM closes shares from one lot (or multiple lots bought on the same UTC day), Date Acquired records that exact YYYY-MM-DD date.
  • Multi-lot across different dates ("Various"): If one disposal consumes FIFO lots bought on two or more different UTC dates, Form 8949 expects Various in the Date Acquired column—matching IRS instructions for aggregated multi-lot sales.
  • Missing prior history ("Unknown"): If a disposal occurs without a preceding acquisition lot in your fetched history, PolyLens flags Date Acquired as Unknown rather than fabricating a purchase date.

2. Short-Term vs. Long-Term Holding Periods

IRS Form 8949 splits capital disposals into Part I (Short-Term, held 1 year or less) and Part II (Long-Term, held more than 1 year).

  • Long-Term: Assigned when all matched FIFO lots in the disposal were held strictly longer than 365 days (soldAt - acquiredAt > 365 days).
  • Short-Term: Assigned when matched lots were held 365 days or less (or when a multi-lot disposal mixes short-term and unknown/mixed lots). Most event contracts settle within days or months, making them Short-Term.

3. Preserving Losing CTF Redemptions ($0 Proceeds Capital Losses)

When a Polymarket outcome resolves against your shares, there is often a $0 USDC payout on-chain. Standard block-explorer CSVs ignore $0 transfers—causing you to pay tax on gross wins without deducting your losses.

  • Losing REDEEM rows: PolyLens closes the remaining open FIFO lots with Proceeds (USD) = 0.00 and records the full negative Gain or (Loss) (USD) (for example, -120.50) so capital losses properly offset capital gains.
  • Rewards & rebates excluded from 8949: Liquidity REWARD, MAKER_REBATE, and TAKER_REBATE inflows are ordinary income, not capital asset disposals. PolyLens excludes them from the Form 8949 CSV while keeping them in your Koinly CSV and Accountant Detailed Ledger.
