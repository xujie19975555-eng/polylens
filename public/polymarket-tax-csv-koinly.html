1. Why Closed-Position & Raw Wallet Syncs Break Polymarket Taxes

  • Missing purchase timestamps: Summary endpoints like /closed-positions omit entry dates, breaking short-term vs. long-term holding rules.
  • Zero-cost-basis redemptions: On-chain CTF REDEEM payouts look like unexplained incoming USDC if the original BUY, SPLIT, or MERGE lots are not matched chronologically.
  • Dropped losing markets: Expired losing YES/NO shares often produce $0 on-chain transfers, causing naive CSVs to omit real capital losses that should offset winning trades.

2. How PolyLens Reconstructs Chronological FIFO Lots

PolyLens queries Polymarket's chronological /v2/activity ledger per wallet and simulates FIFO lot queues for every market outcome token:

  • Buys & Splits: Open FIFO cost-basis lots in USDC at exact UTC execution timestamps.
  • Sells & Merges: Dequeue FIFO lots in order and compute realized profit or loss net of matched basis.
  • Winning & Losing Redemptions: Settle remaining open shares against resolution payouts—explicitly recording negative realized losses when a market resolves against your shares.
  • Rewards & Rebates: Tagged separately as reward events with real transaction hashes.
