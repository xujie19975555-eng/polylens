2. Why Polymarket Traders Get Over-Taxed Without FIFO Reconstruction

  • No brokerage 1099 summary: Because trades execute against Polygon Conditional Token Framework (CTF) contracts from your self-custodied or proxy wallet, you are responsible for reconstructing every acquisition and disposal.
  • Zero-cost-basis inflation: Importing a raw Polygon address into crypto tax tools frequently treats winning REDEEM payouts as unexplained USDC deposits—taxing 100% of gross payouts instead of net profit above cost basis.
  • Missing losing redemptions: When an outcome settles to $0.00, standard token transfers often omit or mislabel the closed shares, preventing capital losses from offsetting winning markets.
  • Separate ordinary rewards: Liquidity REWARD, MAKER_REBATE, and TAKER_REBATE payouts must be separated from capital disposals on IRS Form 8949.
